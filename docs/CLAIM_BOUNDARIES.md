# Claim boundaries

The project makes the following distinctions explicit.

1. **Exact residual-symmetry laws** are mathematical theorems under the stated compact orthogonal compatibility model.
2. **Soft structural risk** is an exact task-weighted bias-variance identity for the stated linear/quadratic estimator and endpoint.
3. **The Gaussian trust certificate** has exact stated coverage only under its Gaussian linear-pilot assumptions.
4. **Neural experiments** test transfer of the theorem-derived statistic; they do not prove that neural residuals are exactly Gaussian.
5. **HELP/HARM on the primary quadratic endpoint** does not imply the same label for retrieval/R@10.
6. **UNCERTAIN** means insufficient evidence. It is not a prediction of NEUTRALITY.
7. The 12-condition deployment audit is a post-hoc synthesis of two separately sealed studies. It is not a preregistered pooled experiment and the 12 conditions are not claimed to be independent datasets.
8. The current prospective neural record contains formal HELP calls and consequential abstention, but no prospectively validated formal HARM certificate.
9. The paper is a supervision-accounting framework, not a claim that the proposed structural estimator is the empirically strongest multimodal alignment method on broad benchmarks.
